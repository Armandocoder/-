# Nafa

- What it is: Nafa is an AI financial assistant app (web + Android test build, iOS in preparation) for West Africa, born in Dakar. The user declares each money movement by voice or text to a conversational AI, which records, categorises, budgets, alerts and produces monthly/annual reports. It NEVER connects automatically to bank or Mobile Money accounts (declarative entry only). It is not a payment app; Wave and Orange Money are channels/partners, not competitors.
- Who it is for: (1) priority: young urban employed adults in Dakar, 25-40, 150-300k FCFA/month, heavy Wave user, no tool today (persona "Aminata"); (2) freelancers/small traders with irregular income who mix business and personal money (persona "Cheikh/Aida"); (3) middle-class people structuring wealth across 3-5+ accounts (persona "Ousmane"), target of the top tier.
- What it sells, at what price: Freemium. Free (0 FCFA, manual tracking + limited features); Premium 1 999 FCFA/month; Ultra 14 999 FCFA/month (adds Goals page, monthly appointment with a certified expert). Customer pays via Mobile Money through PayTech (which adds 2% on top, so 2 038.98 / 15 298.98 FCFA) or via Wave on WhatsApp at exactly 1 999 / 14 999. Possible extra revenue (NOT in the forecasts): sponsored partner offers, shown as ads with consent.
- Where and how: Dakar and large Senegalese cities first, then francophone West Africa (Côte d'Ivoire cited). Interface in French, amounts in FCFA. Acquisition planned via short-form content (TikTok, Instagram), site, communities, referrers. Wolof voice exists but is hidden, not announced.
- Budget and constraints: Seeks 5 000 000 FCFA equity (investments 1 730 000; 3 months working capital 1 900 169; launch cash reserve 1 369 831), no debt. Founder (Armando Gomis, non-developer, Dakar) built the MVP with AI coding agents; supported by La Fabrique 360 – Simplon Sénégal. Company NOT yet registered (no NINEA / RCCM), so PayTech cannot go live: no paying subscriber yet. Domain nafaapp.com (transferred to LWS 15/10/2026).
