# Nafa: the board's summary

## 1. The vote
**1 pass, 2 fund if.** Average score **3.7 / 10** (Offers 4, Monopoly 3, Product 4).
Not a fund today. Two members would fund in stages if the conditions below are met; one says pass on the current evidence.

## 2. Risks raised by more than one member (these go first)
1. **Activation.** 119 of 123 real accounts never recorded one transaction (all three). The first-transaction button is missing on new accounts and fake example numbers flash on login. Every survey percentage is unproven until this is fixed.
2. **Nobody has paid.** Zero payers, no payment test, survey intent from n=14 / 6 / 3 (all three). Conversion, churn and acquisition cost appear nowhere; the 970-payer break-even rests on them.
3. **The habit may not last.** Entering every movement by hand is unproven beyond week one (Product, Monopoly, Offers via effort).
4. **The top tier and best feature are not real yet.** Cash-flow forecasting (most wanted by personas 2 and 3) is not built; Ultra sells a Goals page and one expert appointment with only moderate interest; partner offers are fictional (Offers, Product).
5. **Trust gaps in a money app.** Security audit "refused", free Gemini tier may reuse user content, wrong Ultra price shown, company unregistered so checkout cannot go live (Monopoly, Product, Offers).

## 3. Where they disagree (kept on purpose)
- **Offers and Product see a fixable business; Monopoly sees no durable advantage.** Monopoly: a general AI model behind a declare-and-categorise screen, no network effect, and Wave / Orange Money / Mixx by Yas already hold the data and could ship budgeting. The other two did not weigh this heavily.
- **Narrowing:** Offers wants one buyer (freelancers / traders) and one result (a cash view or forecast). Product wants one thing (declare and see where money goes). Monopoly wants a named first market with a size. Which wins is the founder's call.

## 4. Conditions checklist (merged)
- [ ] Fix activation (first-entry button, fake numbers) and measure: share of new accounts with a first entry, and still active at week 1 and day 30. *(/founder-consumer, /founder-cfo for targets)*
- [ ] Run a real paid test: strangers paying the real price (WhatsApp + Wave is acceptable); the board agrees the threshold in advance. *(/founder-pricing, /founder-offer)*
- [ ] Narrow the launch offer to one buyer and one promised result; add a guarantee the CFO has costed. *(/founder-offer, /founder-cfo)*
- [ ] Build or drop Ultra and the forecast; remove or label all fake elements (partner offers, example numbers, fake admin data). *(/founder-offer)*
- [ ] Fix the Ultra price display (14 704 vs 14 999).
- [ ] Take the AI off the free Gemini tier or tell users plainly what is sent; clear the security audit before any paid launch.
- [ ] Register the company (NINEA, RCCM), get PayTech approved or design manual Wave activation end to end. *(/founder-ops)*
- [ ] Name one durable advantage with evidence and a verified competitor map including the Mobile Money operators' roadmap and Pocket Broker / MyNafa. *(/founder-competitors)*
- [ ] Define the first market with a size and a path to the first 100 payers. *(/founder-marketing, /founder-launch)*
- [ ] Fill the missing numbers (CAC, conversion, churn) with evidence before the plan uses them. *(/founder-cfo)*
- [ ] Release the 5 000 000 FCFA in stages tied to the checks above, not in one payment. *(/founder-cfo)*

## 5. The strongest version the board can see
Smaller than the pitch. Not "a West African wealth assistant with three tiers", but a **voice-first cash notebook for Dakar freelancers and small traders** who already mix business and personal money, use Wave daily and track nothing: say what you earned or spent, see what is really yours this week, then a simple short-term cash forecast. Keep the no-bank-link choice and CDP compliance as the trust story, with concrete proof. Sell one paid tier after a user has seen a result, prove ten to thirty strangers pay by Wave before building Ultra, partners or the Android/RevenueCat work. The young-employee segment is the bigger audience but the weaker reason to pay; the wealth segment (3 cases) is not evidence yet. This differs from the pitch: the pitch leads with young employees and a three-tier suite, the board sees freelancers and a single product.
